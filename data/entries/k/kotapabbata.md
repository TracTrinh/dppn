# Koṭapabbata

## English source

Koṭapabbata (<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Koṭipabbata</span>). A mountain in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Rohaṇa</span>; near it was the village of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kitti</span> (Mhv.xxiii.55). There was a monastery on <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Koṭapabbata</span> called the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Koṭapabbata</span> *vihāra*. This was the residence of the *Sāmaṇera* who was afterwards born as <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Duṭṭhagāmaṇī</span> (Mhv.xxii.25); also of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Summa Thera</span> (Mhv.xxiii.61), of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="8" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Asubhakammikatissa</span> and of his teacher <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="9" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Tissa</span> (Mhv­ṭ.553). The *Vihāra* was near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="10" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Gāma</span> (Mhv­ṭ.553).

The <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="11" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Visuddhimagga</span> (p.292) mentions an Elder, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="12" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Tissa</span>, of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="13" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Koṭapabbata</span> (<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="14" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Koṭipabbata</span>), who, having attained *Arahant*-ship through meditation on breathing, was able to limit the term of his life. According to the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="15" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Dhammapada Commentary</span> (Dhp-a.iv.50), an Elder named <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="16" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anula</span> lived in the monastery during the time of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="17" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Duṭṭhagāmaṇī</span>, and the village <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="18" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Bhokkanta</span>, residence of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="19" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sumanā</span>, wife of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="20" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Lakuṇṭaka Atimbara</span>, was in its vicinity. It was probably the same as <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="21" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Gotapabbata</span> (*q.v.*) (See Mhv.xxxv.124; Mhv­ṭ.667). The <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="22" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Nāgalena</span> was in the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="23" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Koṭapabbata</span> *vihāra* (DN­a.ii.695).

## Vietnamese translation

Koṭapabbata (Koṭipabbata). Một ngọn núi ở Rohaṇa; gần đó là ngôi làng Kitti (Mhv.xxiii.55). Có một tu viện trên Koṭapabbata tên là Koṭapabbata *vihāra* (tu viện). Đây là nơi cư ngụ của vị *Sāmaṇera* (sa-di) mà sau này được tái sanh là Duṭṭhagāmaṇī (Mhv.xxii.25); cũng của Mahā Summa Thera (Mhv.xxiii.61), của Asubhakammikatissa và của thầy ông là Mahā Tissa (Mhv­ṭ.553). *Vihāra* này ở gần Mahā Gāma (Mhv­ṭ.553).

Visuddhimagga (p.292) có đề cập đến một vị Trưởng lão, Tissa, của Koṭapabbata (Koṭipabbata), người đã đắc quả vị Arahant thông qua việc thiền định về hơi thở, đã có thể giới hạn tuổi thọ của mình. Theo Chú giải Dhammapada (Dhp-a.iv.50), một Trưởng lão tên là Anula đã sống trong tu viện vào thời của Duṭṭhagāmaṇī, và ngôi làng Bhokkanta, nơi cư ngụ của Sumanā, vợ của Lakuṇṭaka Atimbara, nằm ở vùng lân cận đó. Nó có lẽ cũng chính là Gotapabbata (*xem mục này*) (Xem Mhv.xxxv.124; Mhv­ṭ.667). Nāgalena nằm trong Koṭapabbata *vihāra* (DN­a.ii.695).
