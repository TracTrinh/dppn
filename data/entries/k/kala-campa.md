# Kāḷa Campā

## English source

Kāḷa Campā. A city in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Aṅga</span>; it was sixty leagues from <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mithilā</span>, with which it was connected by a cart-road (Ja.vi.31f). To <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kāḷa Campā</span> came sages from the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Himālaya</span> to procure salt and sour condiments (Ja.vi.256). There lived <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Puṇṇaka</span>’s relations (Ja.vi.274). <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Varuṇa</span>, the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Nāga</span> king, tells <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="8" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Vidhūra</span> that he (<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="9" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Varuṇa</span>) and his wife were once inhabitants of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="10" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kāḷa Campā</span> (Ja.vi.317). <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="11" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kāḷa Campā</span> is probably another name for <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="12" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Campā</span> (*q.v.*); in fact, the name <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="13" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Campā</span> is used more than once in reference to <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="14" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kāḷa Campā</span> (e.g., Ja.vi.32). According to the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="15" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Aṅguttara Commentary</span> (AN­a.i.131), <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="16" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Soṇa Koḷivisa</span> was born in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="17" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kāḷa Campā</span>; the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="18" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Theragāthā</span> Commentary (Thag­a.i.544), however, speaks of his birthplace as <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="19" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Campā</span>.

## Vietnamese translation

Kāḷa Campā. Một thành phố ở Aṅga; nó cách Mithilā sáu mươi do-tuần, và được kết nối bằng một con đường xe kéo (Ja.vi.31f). Những bậc hiền triết từ Himālaya đã đến Kāḷa Campā để mua muối và gia vị chua (Ja.vi.256). Có những người họ hàng của Puṇṇaka sống ở đó (Ja.vi.274). Varuṇa, vua Nāga, nói với Vidhūra rằng ông (Varuṇa) và vợ mình từng là cư dân của Kāḷa Campā (Ja.vi.317). Kāḷa Campā có lẽ là một tên gọi khác của Campā (*q.v.*); trên thực tế, cái tên Campā được sử dụng nhiều hơn một lần để nói đến Kāḷa Campā (ví dụ, Ja.vi.32). Theo Aṅguttara Commentary (AN­a.i.131), Soṇa Koḷivisa được sinh ra ở Kāḷa Campā; tuy nhiên, Theragāthā Commentary (Thag­a.i.544) lại nói về nơi sinh của ông là Campā.
