# Kehella

## English source

Kehella. A village in Ceylon, the revenue from which <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Aggabodhi III</span>. gave to the *padhānaghara* called <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahallarāja</span>. Cv.xliv.120.
