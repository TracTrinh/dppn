# Laṅkātilaka 03

## English source

Laṅkātilaka 03. A monastery near the modern Kandy, restored by <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Parakkamabāhu VI</span>. (Cv.xci.30). It was built by <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Bhuvanekabāhu IV</span>. Codr.83.

## Vietnamese translation

Laṅkātilaka 03. Một tu viện gần Kandy ngày nay, được Parakkamabāhu VI. trùng tu (Cv.xci.30). Nó được Bhuvanekabāhu IV. xây dựng. Codr.83.
