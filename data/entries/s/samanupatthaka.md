# Samaṇupaṭṭhāka

## English source

Samaṇupaṭṭhāka. Twenty-three *kappas* ago there were four kings of this name, previous births of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Buddhūpaṭṭhāyaka Thera</span>. Ap.i.242.

## Vietnamese translation

Samaṇupaṭṭhāka. Hai mươi ba *kappas* trước có bốn vị vua mang tên này, là những kiếp trước của Buddhūpaṭṭhāyaka Thera. Ap.i.242.
