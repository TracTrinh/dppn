# Soṇa 06

## English source

Soṇa 06. An *Arahant* monk who was sent with <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Uttara</span> to convert <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Suvaṇṇabhūmi</span>. Dpv.viii.12; Vin-a.i.68, 69; Mhv.xii.6, 44ff.; for details see <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Suvaṇṇabhūmi</span>.

## Vietnamese translation

Soṇa 06. Một tỳ-khưu *Arahant* được cử cùng với Uttara để hóa độ Suvaṇṇabhūmi. Dpv.viii.12; Vin-a.i.68, 69; Mhv.xii.6, 44ff.; chi tiết xem Suvaṇṇabhūmi.
