# Sītavana

## English source

Sītavana. A grove near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Rājagaha</span> where <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anāthapiṇḍika</span> first met <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Gotama Buddha</span>. In the grove was a cemetery described as *bhayabherava* (Thag­a.i.47; cf. Divy.264, 268), and, when <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anāthapiṇḍika</span> approached it, he was filled with fear and trembling. But he was reassured by a friendly <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Yakkha</span>, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sīvaka</span> (Vin.ii.155f.; when the Buddha was staying there, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Māra</span> asked him to die; DN.ii.116).

In the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="8" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sītavana</span> was the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="9" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sappasoṇḍikapabbhāra</span> (SN.i.210f; Vin.ii.76; iv.159), where <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="10" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Upasena</span> was killed by a snake bite (SN.iv.40) and <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="11" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Soṇa Koḷivisa</span> tried, without success, to practise asceticism (AN.iii.374). <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="12" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sambhūta Thera</span> so loved the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="13" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sītavana</span> that he came to be called “*Sītavaniya*.” There were five hundred “walks” (*caṅkamanāni*) in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="14" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sītavana</span>. AN­a.ii.679.

In <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="15" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Asoka</span>’s day his brother <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="16" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Tissakumāra</span> (<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="17" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ekavihāriya</span>), is also mentioned as delighting in the solitude of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="18" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sītavana</span> (Thag.vs.540; or does this <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="19" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sītavana</span> not refer to any particular place?).

## Vietnamese translation

Sītavana. Một khu rừng gần Rājagaha, nơi Anāthapiṇḍika lần đầu gặp Gotama Buddha. Trong khu rừng có một bãi tha ma được mô tả là *bhayabherava* (Thag­a.i.47; cf. Divy.264, 268), và, khi Anāthapiṇḍika đến gần đó, ông tràn đầy sợ hãi và run rẩy. Nhưng ông đã được một Yakkha thân thiện, Sīvaka, trấn an (Vin.ii.155f.; khi Đức Phật đang lưu trú tại đó, Māra đã yêu cầu Ngài chết; DN.ii.116).

Trong Sītavana có Sappasoṇḍikapabbhāra (SN.i.210f; Vin.ii.76; iv.159), nơi Upasena bị rắn cắn chết (SN.iv.40) và Soṇa Koḷivisa đã cố gắng, nhưng không thành công, thực hành khổ hạnh (AN.iii.374). Sambhūta Thera yêu thích Sītavana đến mức ngài được gọi là "Sītavaniya." Có năm trăm "lối kinh hành" (*caṅkamanāni*) trong Sītavana. AN­a.ii.679.

Vào thời Asoka, em trai ngài là Tissakumāra (Ekavihāriya), cũng được nhắc đến là ưa thích sự tịch liêu của Sītavana (Thag.vs.540; hay Sītavana này không ám chỉ một địa điểm cụ thể nào?).
