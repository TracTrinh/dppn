# Soṇagiri

## English source

Soṇagiri, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Soṇṇagiri</span>. A mountain district in Ceylon. King <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Cūḷi Mahā Tissa</span> once worked in a sugar mill there for three years in order to earn money wherewith to give alms (Mhv.xxxiv.4). Near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Soṇṇagiri</span> was the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Pipphalī</span> *vihāra* (Vibh­a.439). According to the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Vaṁsaṭīkā</span> (Mhv­ṭ.624; see also Mhv.Trs.238, n.1), <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sonnagiri</span> was part of the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ambaṭṭhakola</span>-range.

## Vietnamese translation

Soṇagiri, Soṇṇagiri. Một vùng núi ở Tích Lan. Vua Mahā Cūḷi Mahā Tissa một lần làm thợ trong một nhà máy đường ở đó ba năm để kiếm tiền cúng dường (Mhv.xxxiv.4). Gần Soṇṇagiri là *vihāra* Pipphali (Vibh­a.439). Theo Mahā Vaṃsaṭīkā (Mhv­ṭ.624; xem cũng Mhv.Trs.238, n.1), Sonnagiri là một phần của dãy Ambaṭṭhakola.
