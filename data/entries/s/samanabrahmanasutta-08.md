# Samaṇabrāhmaṇasutta 08

## English source

Samaṇabrāhmaṇasutta 08. Same as (6), but the controlling powers are different – case, etc. SN.v.208.

## Vietnamese translation

Samaṇabrāhmaṇasutta 08. Giống như (6), nhưng các căn chi phối thì khác nhau – trường hợp, v.v. SN.v.208.
