# Saṅghapāla 01

## English source

Saṅghapāla 01. A *pariveṇa*, residence of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Goṭhābhaya</span> Thera (xxxvi.114). The <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Vaṁsa</span> Commentary (Mhv­ṭ. 673) calls it Saṅghapālaṅgaṇa.

## Vietnamese translation

Saṅghapāla 01. Một *pariveṇa*, nơi cư trú của Goṭhābhaya Thera (xxxvi.114). Chú giải Mahā Vaṁsa (Mhv-ṭ. 673) gọi nó là Saṅghapālaṅgaṇa.
