# Mahallakanāga

## English source

Mahallakanāga, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahallanāga</span>. Father-in-law and commander in-chief of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Gajabāhuka Gāmaṇi</span>. After <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Gajabāhu</span>’s death he became king of Ceylon and reigned for six years (196-202 CE.). He built seven *vihāras*: <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sejalaka</span>, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Goṭapabbata</span>, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Dakapāsāṇa</span>, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sālipabbata</span>, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="8" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Tanaveli</span>, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="9" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Tobbalanāgāpabbata</span> and <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="10" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Girihālika</span>. He was succeeded by his son <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="11" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Bhātikatissa</span>. Mv.xxxv.123ff.; xxxvi.1; Mhv­ṭ.657; Dpv.xxii.15ff., 29.

## Vietnamese translation

Mahallakanāga, Mahallanāga. Cha vợ và là vị tổng tư lệnh của Gajabāhuka Gāmaṇi. Sau cái chết của Gajabāhu, ông đã trở thành vua của Tích Lan và cai trị trong sáu năm (196-202 CN). Ông đã xây dựng bảy *vihāra*: Sejalaka, Goṭapabbata, Dakapāsāṇa, Sālipabbata, Tanaveli, Tobbalanāgāpabbata và Girihālika. Người kế vị ông là con trai của ông, Bhātikatissa. Mv.xxxv.123ff.; xxxvi.1; Mhv­ṭ.657; Dpv.xxii.15ff., 29.
