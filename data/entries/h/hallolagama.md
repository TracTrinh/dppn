# Hallolagāma

## English source

Hallolagāma. A *Caṇḍāla* village near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anurādhapura</span>, where <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Asokamālā</span> was born (Ras.ii.117). Elsewhere it is described as being near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Gama</span> (Ras.ii.125).

## Vietnamese translation

Hallolagāma. Một ngôi làng *Caṇḍāla* gần Anurādhapura, nơi Asokamālā sinh ra (Ras.ii.117). Ở nơi khác, làng này được mô tả là gần Mahā Gama (Ras.ii.125).
