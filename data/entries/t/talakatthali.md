# Taḷākatthalī

## English source

Taḷākatthalī, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Taḷātthala</span>. A locality not far from <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Pulatthipura</span>. It had a fortress which was once occupied by *Laṅkādhinātha* <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Rakkha</span>. Cv.lxx.107, 112, 174.

## Vietnamese translation

Taḷākatthalī, Taḷātthala. Một địa phương cách Pulatthipura không xa. Nó có một pháo đài mà từng bị chiếm giữ bởi *Laṅkādhinātha* Rakkha. Cv.lxx.107, 112, 174.
