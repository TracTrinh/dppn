# Vaṅguttara

## English source

Vaṅguttara. A mountain in Ceylon on which was built the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Pācīnapabbata</span> *vihāra* by <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sūratissa</span> (Mhv.xxi.5). The <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Vaṁsaṭīkā</span> (p. 424) explains that Vaṅguttara was at the foot of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ekadvārika</span> *pabbata*.

## Vietnamese translation

Vaṅguttara. Một ngọn núi ở Tích Lan (Ceylon) nơi Sūratissa cho xây dựng Pācīnapabbata *vihāra* (Mhv.xxi.5). Mahā Vaṁsaṭīkā (p. 424) giải thích rằng Vaṅguttara nằm ở chân núi Ekadvārika *pabbata*.
