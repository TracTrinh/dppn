# Vāsabhagāma

## English source

Vāsabhagāma. A village in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kāsī</span>. It was once the residence of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kassapa Thera</span> (*q.v.*) (Vin.i.312f). Beyond this village was another, called <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Cundaṭṭhila</span>, between <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Vāsabhagāma</span> and <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Benares</span>. Pv.iii.1; Pv-a. 168,170.

## Vietnamese translation

Một ngôi làng ở Kāsī. Nơi đây từng là nơi cư ngụ của Kassapa Thera (*q.v.*) (Vin.i.312f). Vượt qua ngôi làng này là một ngôi làng khác, tên là Cundaṭṭhila, nằm giữa Vāsabhagāma và Benares. Pv.iii.1; Pv-a. 168,170.
