# Vihāravāpi

## English source

Vihāravāpi. A village in Ceylon, near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Tulādhāra</span> *pabbata*. It was the birthplace of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Labhiya Vasabha</span>. Mhv.xxiii.90.

## Vietnamese translation

Vihāravāpi. Một ngôi làng ở Tích Lan, gần *pabbata* Tulādhāra. Đây là nơi sinh của Labhiya Vasabha. Mhv.xxiii.90.
