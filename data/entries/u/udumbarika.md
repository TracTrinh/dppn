# Udumbarikā

## English source

Udumbarikā. A queen (*devī*) who built the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Udumbarikā</span> *Paribbājakārāma* near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Rājagaha</span> (DN.iii.36; DN­a.iii.832).

Close to the *ārāma* was the lotus-pond <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sumāgadhā</span> and a feeding ground for peacocks (<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Moranivāpa</span>) (DN.iii.39).

It was here that the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Udumbarikasīhanādasutta</span> was preached.

## Vietnamese translation

Một hoàng hậu (*devī*), người đã xây dựng Udumbarikā *Paribbājakārāma* gần Rājagaha (DN.iii.36; DN­a.iii.832).

Gần *ārāma* là hồ sen Sumāgadhā và một nơi cho chim công ăn (*Moranivāpa*) (DN.iii.39).

Chính tại đây mà Udumbarikasīhanādasutta đã được thuyết giảng.
