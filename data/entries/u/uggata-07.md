# Uggata 07

## English source

Uggata 07. Fourteen *kappas* ago there were four kings named <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Uggata</span>, previous births of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Parappasādaka</span> (Ap.i.114) or <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Bhūta</span> (Thag­a.i.494) Thera.

## Vietnamese translation

Uggata 07. Mười bốn *kappa* trước có bốn vị vua tên là Uggata, những tiền kiếp của Trưởng lão Parappasādaka (Ap.i.114) hoặc Trưởng lão Bhūta (Thag­a.i.494).
