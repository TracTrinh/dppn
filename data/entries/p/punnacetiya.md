# Puññacetiya

## English source

Puññacetiya. A *cetiya* attached to the monastery where <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ariyavaṁsa</span> wrote the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Maṇisāramañjūsā</span>. Bode, 42.

## Vietnamese translation

Puññacetiya. Một *cetiya* gắn liền với tu viện nơi Ariyavaṁsa đã viết Maṇisāramañjūsā. Bode, 42.
