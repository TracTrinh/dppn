# Pāsāṇasinna

## English source

Pāsāṇasinna. A locality in Ceylon where <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Dhātusena</span> built the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Dhātusenapabbata</span> *vihāra*. Cv.xxxviii.47.

## Vietnamese translation

Pāsāṇasinna. Một địa phương ở Tích Lan nơi Dhātusena đã xây dựng *vihāra* Dhātusenapabbata. Cv.xxxviii.47.
