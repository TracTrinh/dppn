# Gaṅgarājī

## English source

Gaṅgarājī. A district to the east of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anurādhapura</span>, where <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Kaniṭṭhatissa</span> built the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anulatissapabbata</span> *vihāra*. Mhv.xxxvi.15.

## Vietnamese translation

Gaṅgarājī. Một vùng ở phía đông Anurādhapura, nơi Kaniṭṭhatissa đã xây dựng vihāra Anulatissapabbata. Mhv.xxxvi.15.
