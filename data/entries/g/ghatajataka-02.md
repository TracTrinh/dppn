# Ghatajātaka 02

## English source

Ghatajātaka 02. (Ja 454). The story of the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Andhakaveṇhudāsaputta</span> (*q.v.*) and of the manner in which <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ghata</span> *paṇḍita* assuaged the grief of his brother, <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Vāsudeva</span>. The introductory story resembles that of the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Maṭṭhakundalijātaka</span>.

<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Rohiṇeyya</span> is identified with <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ānanda</span> and <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Vāsudeva</span> with <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="8" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Sāriputta</span> (Ja.iv.79ff).

This <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="9" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Jātaka</span> perhaps influenced the story of <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="10" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Ummādacittā</span> found in the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="11" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Maha Vaṁsa</span> (ix.13); for its connection with the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="12" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Harivaṁsa</span>, see ZDMG.53, 25ff.

## Vietnamese translation

Ghatajātaka 02. (Ja 454). Câu chuyện về các Andhakaveṇhudāsaputta (*q.v.*) và về cách mà *paṇḍita* Ghata xoa dịu nỗi đau buồn của người anh trai mình, Vāsudeva. Câu chuyện giới thiệu tương tự như câu chuyện của Maṭṭhakundalijātaka.

Rohiṇeyya được nhận diện là Ānanda và Vāsudeva là Sāriputta (Ja.iv.79ff).

Jātaka này có lẽ đã ảnh hưởng đến câu chuyện về Ummādacittā được tìm thấy trong Maha Vaṁsa (ix.13); về mối liên hệ của nó với Harivaṁsa, xem ZDMG.53, 25ff.
