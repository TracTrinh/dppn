# Gajakumbhakapāsāṇa

## English source

Gajakumbhakapāsāṇa. A locality in Ceylon, through which passed the *Sīmā* marked out by <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Devānampiyatissa</span> for the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Mahā Vihāra</span>. Mhv., p.332, v.12; Dpv.xiv.35; Mbv.135.

## Vietnamese translation

Gajakumbhakapāsāṇa. Một địa danh ở Tích Lan, nơi ranh giới *Sīmā* do Devānampiyatissa vạch ra cho Mahā Vihāra đi qua. Mhv., p.332, v.12; Dpv.xiv.35; Mbv.135.
