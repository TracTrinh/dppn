# Gāmaṇivāpi

## English source

Gāmaṇivāpi. A tank near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Anurādhapura</span>. Near it was a hermitage built by <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Paṇḍukābhaya</span>. Mhv.x.96; see Mhv.Trs., p.75, n.1.

## Vietnamese translation

Gāmaṇivāpi. Một hồ chứa nước gần Anurādhapura. Gần đó có một am thất do Paṇḍukābhaya xây dựng. Mhv.x.96; xem Mhv.Trs., p.75, n.1.
