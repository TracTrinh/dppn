# Goṭhakasamudda

## English source

Goṭhakasamudda. The sea near Ceylon, the “shallow sea.” Mhv.xxii.49, 85; DN­a.ii.695.

## Vietnamese translation

Goṭhakasamudda. Vùng biển gần Tích Lan, "biển cạn". Mhv.xxii.49, 85; DN­a.ii.695.
