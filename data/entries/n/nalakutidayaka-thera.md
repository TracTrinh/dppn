# Naḷakuṭidāyaka Thera

## English source

Naḷakuṭidāyaka Thera. An *Arahant*. Thirty-one *kappas* ago he built a house of reeds near <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="1" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Bhārika</span> (<span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="2" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Hārita</span>) in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="3" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Himavā</span>, for the <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="4" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Pacceka Buddha</span> <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="5" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Nārada</span>, thatched it with reeds and made a covered walk near by. When he was born in <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="6" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Tāvatiṁsa</span> he had a palace sixty leagues in extent (Ap.ii.440). He is probably identical with <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="7" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Valliya</span> Thera (Thag­a.i.247). The same verses are attributed to <span class="dppn-term dppn-term-t14" data-dppn-term-pos="8" data-dppn-term-class="t14">Naḷagārika</span>.

## Vietnamese translation

Naḷakuṭidāyaka Thera. Một *Arahant*. Ba mươi mốt kappa trước, ngài đã xây một túp lều bằng lau sậy gần Bhārika (Hārita) ở Himavā cho Pacceka Buddha Nārada, lợp mái bằng lau sậy và làm một con đường kinh hành có mái che gần đó. Khi tái sinh ở Tāvatiṁsa, ngài có một cung điện rộng sáu mươi do-tuần (Ap.ii.440). Ngài có lẽ chính là Valliya Thera (Thag­a.i.247). Các vần kệ tương tự cũng được gán cho Naḷagārika.
